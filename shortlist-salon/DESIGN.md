Shortlist Salon is styled as a prize-season reading room: a warm parchment page, deep library-green masthead, oxblood ribbon and brass rules, so a book club feels it is holding a literary object rather than a dashboard. Serif type (Iowan Old Style/Palatino) carries titles and numerals while small tracked sans labels handle data, matching how printed programmes and dust jackets separate voice from information. Each book gets a stable cloth-spine colour and mock cover that follows it through lists, plans and progress bars, so readers recognise titles by colour before text. Oxblood is reserved for "behind" and errors, brass for the leading prediction and the countdown timeline, making urgency legible without alarm-red dashboards. Forms, hit targets and layouts are sized for phones at 390px and stay single-column until there is room for a sticky side form, with visible focus rings and inline errors under each field.
