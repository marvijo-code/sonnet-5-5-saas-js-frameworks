Rowguard Almanac is styled as a farmer's almanac: warm parchment paper, vine-green ink and serif headings, because its users are growers who trust printed field calendars more than dashboards. The palette borrows from the spotted lanternfly itself (tan forewing, red hindwing, black spots), so the red is reserved for real alerts and never used decoratively. Risk is shown as a stamped seal plus a ruled 0–100 gauge with ticks at 30 and 60, with a text tier label so meaning never depends on colour alone. Lists are ledger-style cards with a coloured spine, date chips and roman-numeral section tabs, which keeps dense records scannable at 390px and 1120px alike. Destructive actions apply instantly with an Undo toast, and all type is system-serif and system-sans so the product needs no network requests.
