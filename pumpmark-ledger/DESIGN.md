Pumpmark Ledger looks like a forecourt at night: a graphite ledger with amber pump-display digits, because fleet managers scan money figures before anything else. Every form is a cream thermal-receipt slip with torn edges, so entering data feels like handling a fuel docket rather than filling in an admin panel. Serif headings, monospaced tabular numerals and hairline ledger rules give the numbers an accounting-book credibility that generic dashboard cards lack. Amber means "your money", hot coral means "over budget", and the budget bars carry a hard limit tick so overspend is visible without reading. The layout collapses to a single column at phone width and keeps 44px touch targets, since drivers and dispatchers use it from the yard.
