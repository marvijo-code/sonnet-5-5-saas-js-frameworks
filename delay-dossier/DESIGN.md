Delay Dossier is designed as a manila case folder, with paper-grid backgrounds, folder tabs for navigation and a navy masthead, so a disruption claim feels like a file being built rather than a form being filled. Each trip is a boarding-pass ticket with a dashed tear-off stub and monospaced flight and airport codes, which frequent flyers and agents already read at a glance. Colour carries meaning only: teal for eligible or paid, amber for warnings and the brand accent, red for extraordinary causes, rejections and deadlines under 30 days. Serif display type gives the tool a legal, trustworthy voice while monospace labels keep data scannable. The unclaimed-compensation ticket sits in the header on every screen because that figure is the reason to act now.
